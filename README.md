# NovaStream for Android TV and Fire TV

A TV player for your own provider's channels, movies and series. NovaStream has no channels of its own:
you sign in with the login your provider gave you.

**Latest version: 1.0.34** · [Download NovaStream.apk](https://github.com/grivera82/novastream-tv/releases/latest/download/NovaStream.apk)

## Install on a Fire TV

1. **Get Downloader.** On the Fire TV home screen, open **Find** (the magnifying glass), search for
   **Downloader** (the orange app by AFTVnews) and install it.
2. **Allow Downloader to install apps.** Go to **Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › Install unknown
   apps** and turn **Downloader** on. No Developer options? Go to **Settings › My Fire TV › About**, click the
   Fire TV's name **7 times** until it says you're a developer, then go back.
3. **Download NovaStream.** Open Downloader, allow what it asks for, type **9760489** in the address box and
   press **Go**.
4. **Install it.** Choose **Install**, then **Done**. When Downloader offers to delete the file, choose
   **Delete**: the app is already installed.
5. **Open NovaStream.** It's under **Apps**. Choose **Add a playlist** and enter the server, username and
   password from your provider.

Updates come by themselves: NovaStream says when a new version is out and installs it when you agree.

## Instalar en un Fire TV

1. **Instala Downloader.** En la pantalla de inicio, abre **Buscar** (la lupa), busca **Downloader** (la app
   naranja de AFTVnews) e instálala.
2. **Permite que Downloader instale apps.** Ve a **Configuración › Mi Fire TV › Opciones para desarrolladores ›
   Instalar apps desconocidas** y activa **Downloader**. ¿No ves Opciones para desarrolladores? Ve a
   **Configuración › Mi Fire TV › Acerca de**, pulsa **7 veces** el nombre del Fire TV y vuelve atrás.
3. **Descarga NovaStream.** Abre Downloader, acepta lo que pida, escribe **9760489** y pulsa **Go**.
4. **Instálala.** Elige **Instalar** y luego **Listo**. Cuando Downloader ofrezca borrar el archivo, elige
   **Eliminar**: la app ya está instalada.
5. **Abre NovaStream.** Está en **Apps**. Elige **Add a playlist** y escribe el servidor, el usuario y la
   contraseña de tu proveedor.

Las actualizaciones llegan solas: NovaStream avisa cuando hay una versión nueva y la instala si aceptas.
